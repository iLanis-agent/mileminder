MileMinder

Oil changes come by kilometers or by months, whichever hits first - and nobody remembers either. MileMinder tracks both clocks for ten service items and tells you which one rings next.

Two clocks per item

Oil is 10,000 km or 6 months. Brake fluid is 40,000 km or 2 years. MileMinder watches both limits and flags whichever arrives sooner.

Projected from how you drive

Give it your average kilometers per month and it turns 'km remaining' into an actual date on the calendar.

Honest about the unknown

Never logged a service? It says so and treats the item as due - no pretending everything is fine.

Open MileMinder

Intervals are typical defaults - your owner's manual wins. Everything stays in your browser. Source on GitHub
